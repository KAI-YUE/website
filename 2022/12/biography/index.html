Building Products, Understanding Customers

September 11, 2026 in Biography

I’ve built a paid investment education community and now lead technical review and acceptance for an AI-agent platform serving marketplace sellers. My work starts with understanding what people need and taking responsibility for what gets delivered.

Work

  • AI systems and engineering delivery. Guide technical direction, evaluate external engineering proposals, review architecture and code, and make technical acceptance decisions. The CEO retains final business approval.
  • Investment education community. Built a YouTube audience that peaked above 9,000 subscribers and remains above 7,500 as of September 2026, alongside a private Telegram community offered at $30/month. Taught members to explore investment questions through code.
  • Game development. Developing an indie game, currently refining its core mechanics and exploring how a story can express my philosophy.

Writing

What Remains Valuable When Answers Become Cheap? — reflections on running an education community, developing a game, and guiding engineering delivery as AI changes what is easy to produce.

Working Together

I’m interested in joining an ambitious early-stage team where I can build, shape product decisions, and take responsibility for delivery. I’m looking for teammates who care about commercial viability and the effect their products have on people.
